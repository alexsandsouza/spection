# New project: Discover → Specify → Architect → Plan

1. Interview stakeholders; distinguish business evidence from assumptions.
2. Complete Product Brief with explicit in/out scope and success measures.
3. Define personas, journeys, RF/RNF requirements and Given/When/Then criteria.
4. Create HLD, LLD and ADRs for material architecture trade-offs.
5. Review privacy, security, accessibility and operations needs.
6. Identify first vertical slice, generate feature spec, plan and tasks.
7. Obtain human review. Implementation only after relevant requirements are reviewed.

**Exit evidence:** approved specification references, open questions, owners and traceability links.
