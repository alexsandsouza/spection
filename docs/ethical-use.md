# Ethical and Responsible Use

SPECTION supports accountable software engineering, not unchecked automation. These guidelines supplement, and do not restrict, the MIT license.

## Principles
- Human accountability: identify a responsible owner; review consequential decisions and provide recourse.
- Privacy: minimize data, avoid secrets in prompts and logs, review provider retention and transfers.
- Fairness and accessibility: evaluate foreseeable harms, representative cases and inclusive user journeys.
- Transparency: disclose material AI use and limitations; do not misrepresent generated content or test evidence.
- Security: enforce least privilege outside the model, validate outputs and require approval for high-impact tool actions.
- Intellectual property: review licensing and attribution of generated and third-party material.
- Sustainability: measure cost, latency, energy-relevant resource use and choose proportionate solutions.

## Release checklist
- [ ] Requirements and acceptance criteria reviewed.
- [ ] Threat model and privacy assessment reviewed.
- [ ] Applicable tests executed with evidence.
- [ ] Model evaluations and misuse cases reviewed when AI is integrated.
- [ ] Accessibility, fairness and human escalation considered.
- [ ] Rollback, monitoring, incident owner and release approval recorded.

Report vulnerabilities privately when possible; avoid posting secrets or personal data in public issues. The framework is not a certification or legal guarantee. See [responsible development](responsible-development.md) and [security guidance](../kit/docs/security-ethics.md).
